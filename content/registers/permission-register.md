## Obligations that travel with the licence — `DEC-019`

`CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO` is **not** "free to use". Three obligations attach, and they are consequences
of using this source, not paperwork:

| Obligation | What it means here |
|---|---|
| **BY** — attribution | Every derived item carries `issuingBody` and `sourceDocument`. The attribution is in the data, not in a footer someone can redesign away |
| **NC** — non-commercial | ⚠️ **Citadel cannot charge for access to this content.** `DEC-019` settled that Citadel is non-commercial; this source is one of the reasons that decision is now load-bearing rather than philosophical |
| **SA** — share-alike | Derived content inherits the licence. It cannot be relicensed more restrictively later |
